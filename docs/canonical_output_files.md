e-Invoicing — EN 16931 (UBL 2.1 or CII) + official validators

What to emit: UBL 2.1 <Invoice> XML (or UN/CEFACT CII) conforming to EN 16931 core model; prefer Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (a CIUS of EN 16931) to inherit strict business rules.
